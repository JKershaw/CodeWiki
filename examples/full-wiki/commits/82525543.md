# Structure Agent and Wiki Organization System

The Structure Agent is a meta-level agent responsible for analyzing the overall organization and quality of the wiki as it grows. Unlike analysis agents that focus on individual commits, the Structure Agent examines the wiki as a whole, identifying organizational issues, redundancies, gaps, and opportunities for better content structure. It operates periodically to ensure the wiki maintains high quality and coherent organization as new content is added.

The agent works by examining all existing wiki pages, their relationships, and their coverage of the codebase. It identifies several types of issues: duplicate content that should be merged, pages that are too broad and should be split, gaps where important topics lack documentation, and organizational improvements like better categorization or cross-referencing. Each finding includes an importance rating (low, medium, high) to help prioritize remediation work.

A key enhancement introduced alongside the Structure Agent is improved wiki page naming. The system now generates concise, descriptive titles (3-6 words) that clearly communicate the content's purpose, such as "Multi-Agent Architecture" or "Web Interface Design" rather than technical commit-based names. This makes the wiki more navigable and professional, reading like a proper encyclopedia rather than a collection of commit summaries. The narrative agent now explicitly generates these titles during commit analysis, and the orchestrator uses them when creating wiki pages.
